FillFinder

● Live

Finding ADHD meds during a national shortage.

During the stimulant shortage, the only way to find a pharmacy that actually has your medication in stock is to call around — shelf-level inventory just isn't public anywhere. FillFinder fixes that: track your run-out dates and call log privately, check live FDA shortage status, and tap into anonymous, crowd-sourced stock reports by location and medication. No account needed — a random device ID keeps your reports yours.

decision-memory

● Open source

Stop losing the "why" behind your code.

Every codebase accumulates decisions that aren't obvious from the code — the context lives in PR threads, chat history, and people's heads, and new devs (or AI assistants) end up repeating old mistakes. decision-memory captures architectural choices as living markdown files and surfaces them as advisories right when you edit the relevant code. A nudge, not a blocker — available via CLI, slash commands, and an MCP server.
